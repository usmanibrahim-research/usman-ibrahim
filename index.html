09 October 2026

Reinforcement learning-based EWMA paper available online

Our paper, “A reinforcement learning-based EWMA control chart with an application to urban mobility monitoring,” is available online in Computers & Industrial Engineering. Co-authored with Mohammad AlDurgam, Nasir Abbas, and Alaa Khamis, the study learns signaling decisions using reinforcement learning and applies the method to Chicago Transit Authority bus ridership data.

Read about the paper ↗

04 October 2026

Joint process monitoring paper published

Our paper, “Joint process monitoring in power plants using conditional aggregate mean control charts,” is published in the Journal of Industrial and Production Engineering. With Nasir Abbas and Muhammad Riaz, we develop charts for monitoring process mean and variance together and apply them to combined-cycle power plant data.

Read about the paper ↗

SMiLE 2026 · Conference Proceedings

Research on risk-based driver allocation

With Samhar Alzghaier and Mohammad AlDurgam, I study the allocation of pre-booked and backup drivers for urban last-mile delivery. The paper develops a stochastic optimization model that accounts for route risk and staffing costs.

Read about the paper ↗

2025

Water pH monitoring paper published

Our paper, “Smart Monitoring Solutions for Real-Time Water pH Regulation in Aquatic Ecotoxicology,” is published in the Journal of Chemometrics. Co-authored with Nasir Abbas, Muhammad Riaz, and Tahir Mahmood, it examines statistical monitoring of water pH in an aquatic ecotoxicology laboratory.

I am interested in methods for detecting changes in process mean and variability, with applications to chemical & industrial processes and smart mobility. My interests also include agentic AI for decision-making and automation..
