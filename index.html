Joint Process Monitoring in Power Plants Using Conditional Aggregate Mean Control Charts

Usman Ibrahim, Nasir Abbas, and Muhammad Riaz

Journal of Industrial and Production Engineering, 2026

DOI: 10.1080/21681015.2026.2737266

Read the paper Publisher page

Overview

This paper introduces joint control charts for monitoring shifts in process mean and variance simultaneously. The charts combine the Conditional Aggregate Mean approach with three variance transformations and are intended to detect a broad range of process shifts.

The method is evaluated through simulation and applied to data from a combined-cycle power plant to illustrate its use in industrial process monitoring.

Topics

Statistical process control; joint mean and variance monitoring; Conditional Aggregate Mean; memory control charts; variance transformations; combined-cycle power plants.
