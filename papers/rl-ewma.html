A reinforcement learning-based EWMA control chart with an application to urban mobility monitoring

Usman Ibrahim, Mohammad AlDurgam, Nasir Abbas & Alaa Khamis

Computers & Industrial Engineering, 223, 112440

Available online 9 October 2026

DOI: 10.1016/j.cie.2026.112440

Free-access link ↗ Publisher page ↗

Abstract

Recent studies have applied reinforcement learning to control chart design, but direct learning of the signaling decision remains limited. In this study, we propose a reinforcement learning-based exponentially weighted moving average control chart for process monitoring. The monitoring task is formulated as a Markov decision process in which a proximal policy optimization agent learns a signaling probability from the current sample observation and EWMA statistic. The chart performance is evaluated using the average run length, expected average run length, and extra quadratic loss over a range of shift magnitudes. The numerical results show that the proposed chart generally provides shorter detection delays for small and moderate shifts, and its minimum extra quadratic loss is lower than the corresponding minima for the adaptive EWMA, classical EWMA, and CUSUM charts. We further illustrate the applicability of the approach through an urban mobility monitoring application using bus ridership data from the Chicago Transit Authority. The case study illustrates the signaling patterns of the chart during pronounced variations in observed ridership, including those around 2007–2008 and 2020.
